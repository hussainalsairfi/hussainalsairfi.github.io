Research profile / Research topics

Thermofluids, CFD, Microgravity and Advanced Materials

Topic page linking thermocapillary droplet-flow simulation, hydrodynamic DCMD enhancement, microgravity two-phase flow and rapid-solidification research on high-entropy alloys.

Research problem

Fluid mechanics, heat transfer, microgravity transport and materials processing share a need for careful numerical or experimental control of interfaces, gradients and microstructure. The research collected here connects VOF-Ansys two-phase-flow modelling, membrane-channel hydrodynamics and rapid solidification of high-entropy alloys.

Who may find this useful?

  • Thermofluids researchers
  • CFD and VOF simulation specialists
  • Microgravity fluid mechanics researchers
  • Membrane-module designers
  • Advanced materials and high-entropy alloy researchers

Questions this topic addresses

  • How do thermocapillary and buoyancy forces compete in confined droplet systems?
  • How can VOF-Ansys simulations support interpretation of two-phase droplet migration under zero and normal gravity?
  • How do corrugated feed channels alter DCMD hydrodynamics, temperature polarization and pressure drop?
  • How does rapid solidification change phase formation and microhardness in equimolar AlCrCuFeNi high-entropy alloy?
