Membrane Distillation for Seawater and Oilfield-Produced Water

Topic page linking research on direct-contact, air-gap and vacuum membrane distillation for seawater desalination, hypersaline feeds and oilfield-produced water treatment.

Research problem

Membrane distillation can treat high-salinity streams that are difficult for pressure-driven desalination processes. The research collected here examines DCMD, AGMD and VMD modules, membrane materials, temperature, salinity, flow rate, channel geometry and gap or vacuum-space effects for seawater and produced-water treatment.

Who may find this useful?

  • Membrane distillation researchers
  • Desalination engineers
  • Produced-water treatment specialists
  • Thermal membrane process modelers
  • Water researchers in arid regions

Questions this topic addresses

  • How do DCMD, AGMD and VMD compare for saline feeds and oilfield-produced water?
  • How do hot-channel depth, cold-channel depth, air gap and vacuum space affect permeate flux and salt rejection?
  • Which membrane materials and design features support robust desalination performance?
  • How can PhD-scale experimental work inform pilot-scale membrane-distillation systems in arid regions?
