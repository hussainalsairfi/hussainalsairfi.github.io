Research profile / Research topics

AI, Renewable Energy and Water-Energy Systems

Topic page connecting AI/ML, photovoltaic performance, air pollution, wind-battery energy management and flexible desalination-load research in arid regions.

Research problem

Renewable energy and desalination are increasingly linked in arid regions, where water scarcity, air pollution, dust, intermittent wind power and battery degradation affect system reliability and cost. The research collected here uses machine learning and adaptive control to quantify PV losses and improve wind-battery desalination operation.

Who may find this useful?

  • Renewable energy researchers
  • Water-energy nexus researchers
  • AI/ML energy-system analysts
  • PV operation and maintenance researchers
  • Desalination system modelers
  • Battery-energy management researchers

Questions this topic addresses

  • How much do air pollution and soiling reduce PV energy yield and project economics in the Arabian Peninsula?
  • How can machine learning estimate clean-atmosphere counterfactual PV performance?
  • How can a reverse-osmosis desalination plant operate as a flexible electrical load?
  • How can adaptive fuzzy control reduce battery degradation in renewable-powered desalination systems?
