An Offline Digital-Twin-Assisted Decision-Support Framework for Dynamic RO Under Kuwait Solar-Availability Conditions

Fajer M. Alelaj, Mohammed A. Bou-Rabee, Mustafa Fadel, Shafqat Aziz, Adil Aslam Mir, Abdulrahman Alharbi, Hussain Al-Sairfi · Membranes · 23 August 2026

AI-readable research summary

This offline framework combines dynamic RO experimental data from NREL with Kuwait solar context from NASA POWER. Machine-learning models predict permeate flow and estimate specific energy consumption, while an operating-point ranking layer supports decisions. It is an offline process-analysis tool; conductivity prediction remains limited.

Plain-language explanation

A virtual analysis tool compares RO operating conditions using experimental process records and solar availability in Kuwait.

Why this work matters: The study connects predictive modeling with renewable desalination decisions while distinguishing offline analysis from live plant control.
