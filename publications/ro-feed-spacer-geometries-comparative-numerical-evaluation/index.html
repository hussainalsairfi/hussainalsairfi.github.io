Membrane-module hydrodynamics and CFD

Comparative Numerical Evaluation of Feed-Spacer Geometries in Reverse Osmosis Modules for Enhanced Water Treatment Sustainability

Hussain Al-Sairfi, Fajer M. Alelaj, Mohammad K. Alhamli, Mustafa Fadel, Hawraa Sabti · Membranes · 10 August 2026

AI-readable research summary

This CFD study compares grid, hexagonal and sinusoidal RO feed spacers in ANSYS Fluent. It examines concentration polarization, wall shear and hydraulic losses. The hexagonal design offers the strongest modeled mass-transfer versus pressure-loss balance; advanced geometries still require experimental validation.

Plain-language explanation

Changing the spacer shape changes how saline water mixes next to the membrane. Computer simulations help compare designs before physical testing.

Why this work matters: The work provides comparative numerical guidance for RO spacer design, with conclusions limited to the simulated channel conditions.
