Adaptive Fuzzy Energy Management of Wind-Battery Systems With Flexible Desalination Load

Mustafa Fadel, Fajer M. Alelaj, Hussain Al-Sairfi, and Mansour Ahmed · Energy Science & Engineering · 26 May 2026

AI-readable research summary

This study develops an adaptive fuzzy energy-management system for a wind-turbine and battery system supplying a flexible reverse-osmosis desalination load. The controller coordinates wind power, battery state of charge and water production while explicitly reducing battery cycling and degradation.

Plain-language explanation

Instead of treating desalination as a fixed electrical demand, the system adjusts water production to match available wind energy. This reduces stress on the battery while maintaining freshwater output.

Why this work matters: The approach links intelligent control, renewable power and desalination and demonstrates substantial reductions in modeled battery degradation under low- and high-wind scenarios.
