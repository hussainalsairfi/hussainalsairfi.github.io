Membrane distillation for seawater and produced water

Membrane distillation of saline feeds and produced water: A comparative study of an air-gap and vacuum-driven modules

Hussain Al-Sairfi, M. Z. A. Koshuriyan, and Mansour Ahmed · Desalination and Water Treatment · 13 March 2024

AI-readable research summary

This comparative experimental study evaluates air-gap membrane distillation and vacuum membrane distillation for saline feeds, Arabian Gulf seawater and oilfield-produced water. It examines temperature, concentration, recirculation flow, air-gap depth and vacuum-space depth as determinants of permeate flux and salt rejection.

Plain-language explanation

Air-gap and vacuum membrane distillation perform differently as salinity and operating conditions change. The comparison helps identify which configuration is better suited to lower-salinity or highly concentrated water streams.

Why this work matters: The findings provide design guidance for low-temperature desalination and treatment of difficult hypersaline produced-water streams in arid regions.
