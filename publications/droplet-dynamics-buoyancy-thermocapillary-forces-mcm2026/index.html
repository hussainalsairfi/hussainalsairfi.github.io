Droplet Dynamics in Small-Scale Containers: The Impact of Buoyancy and Thermocapillary Forces

Yousuf Alhendal; Mohammad K. Alhamli; Hussain Al-Sairfi · MCM 2026 · Paper HTFF 110

Research summary

A numerical study of an FC-75 droplet in silicone oil examines how container size changes the competition between thermocapillary and buoyancy forces. The extended abstract reports Volume-of-Fluid simulations in a differentially heated cylindrical container, with thermocapillary-dominated motion in smaller configurations and stronger buoyancy influence as the container becomes larger.

This is a site summary, not a reproduction of the published extended abstract.

Plain-language explanation

Heating and gravity can move a droplet in opposite directions. This conference contribution uses computer simulations to examine how that balance changes with container size.
