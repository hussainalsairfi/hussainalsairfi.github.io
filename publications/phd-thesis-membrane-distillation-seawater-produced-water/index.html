Membrane distillation for seawater and produced water

Assessment of Membrane Distillation Modules for Seawater Desalination and Oilfield-Produced Water Treatment Applications

Hussain Al-Sairfi · University of Strathclyde doctoral thesis · PhD awarded in 2025

AI-readable research summary

This doctoral thesis experimentally evaluates direct-contact, air-gap and vacuum membrane-distillation modules for seawater, synthetic saline feeds and oilfield-produced water. It examines membrane material, temperature, salinity, flow rate and channel or gap dimensions to establish performance and design guidance under Kuwait-relevant conditions.

Plain-language explanation

The thesis compares three membrane-distillation configurations and identifies the operating conditions that improve water flux while maintaining very high salt rejection for seawater and difficult produced-water streams.

Why this work matters: It consolidates the experimental foundation for Hussain Al-Sairfi’s membrane-distillation research and provides an official full-text doctoral record through the University of Strathclyde repository.
