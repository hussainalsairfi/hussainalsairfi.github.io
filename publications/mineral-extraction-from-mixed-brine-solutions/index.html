Brine chemistry, scaling control and mineral recovery

Mineral Extraction from Mixed Brine Solutions

M. A. Salman, M. Ahmed, Hussain Al-Sairfi, and Y. Al-Foudari · Separations · 1 October 2025

AI-readable research summary

This paper evaluates sulfate-mineral recovery when high-salinity brines are mixed with seawater. A practical model predicts saturation index, precipitation yield and induction time for barite, celestite and calcium-sulfate phases under different mixing and operating conditions.

Plain-language explanation

Brines that create difficult scale can also contain recoverable industrial minerals. The study shows how controlled mixing can make selected sulfate minerals precipitate before they damage desalination or oil-production equipment.

Why this work matters: The results support pretreatment strategies that convert problematic scaling ions into recoverable products while reducing environmental discharge and downstream fouling risk.
